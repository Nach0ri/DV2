4 Key Findings from Our Data

What the rescue data reveal about NSW wildlife.

Rescues cluster in a few councils

Top five councils had 18.8% of 2024–25 rescues.

See Idiom 01

Vehicle collisions are the top reason

15,639 rescues in 2022–23, 2.4 times the next reason.

See Idiom 02

Reptile rescues are the most seasonal

13% of reptile rescues in January, only 2% in July.

See Idiom 10

Died is the largest recorded outcome

54.8% recorded as Died, 37.1% as Released (2013–14 to 2018–19).
